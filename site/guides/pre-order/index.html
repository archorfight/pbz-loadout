Pre-Order Guide: Which Edition to Buy

Editions, prices, bonuses, and store differences — verified, not speculated.

Quick answer: Two editions, same game — Standard $59.99 and Digital Deluxe $69.99. Deluxe adds two exclusive outfits, an artbook and the soundtrack; no gameplay content. Pre-order bonuses are early unlocks you can earn by playing, and there is no early access: everyone starts October 29. Want just the game? Standard.

Are the pre-order bonuses worth it? OFFICIAL

Honest answer: a head start, not exclusive content.

  • Treasure Basin (accessory): +10% Remnant Dust drop rate.
  • Legacy (outfit): cosmetic.

Both "can also be obtained through in-game progression" — that wording appears on the official site, the PlayStation Store listing, and the Steam page. Pre-order because you're buying anyway — not to "lock in" bonuses. And no edition gets you in before the October 29 unlock.

Where to pre-order — platform differences

  • PS5 / PS5 Pro — PlayStation Store. Digital pre-orders since Aug 11, 2026. Pro Enhanced, supports Game Help; PS5 has a 12-month console exclusivity window (no Xbox before late 2027).
  • PC — Steam — AppID 4115450. The safe default: achievements, cloud saves, Family Sharing, refund policy. The page discloses Denuvo DRM.
  • PC — Epic Games Store — same game and editions; community promo bundles have included Fortnite cosmetics (not an official S-GAME benefit — verify before buying).
  • Mainland China PC — WeGame / TapTap PC, same ¥268 / ¥328 pricing.

No store-exclusive weapons or missions exist. The only meaningful choice is which launcher you want the game in.

Pre-order timeline

  • Aug 6, 2026 — S-GAME declares core development complete.

FAQ

Which edition do streamers/speedrunners buy?
Standard — the Treasure Basin accessory (+10% Remnant Dust) matters most for fast progression, and it's in both editions anyway.

Is there a physical edition?
Japan (package edition) and mainland China (limited ¥1,199 collector's box, ~20,000 units, JD store). No global physical release announced.
