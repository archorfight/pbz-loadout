About this site

Phantom Blade Loadout is an unofficial fan resource for Phantom Blade Zero, focused on one thing: the weapon system — 30+ main weapons, 25 Phantom Edges, and the four-slot loadout they form. At launch (October 29, 2026) it becomes a full weapon database with stat comparison and a loadout planner.

Editorial policy: we publish only officially confirmed facts before launch, clearly labeled. We never fabricate stats — a problem already visible across pre-launch coverage. After release, everything here is re-verified against the actual game.
