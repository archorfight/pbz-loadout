Phantom Edges: the 25 Weapons Beside Your Weapons

What S-GAME has confirmed about the series-signature secondary weapon system.

Quick answer: Phantom Edges are 25 secondary weapons — hidden blades, projectiles and utility tools — equipped alongside your two main weapons. You carry two Edges at once, deployable mid-combo and from stealth. The full named list and per-Edge effects arrive on launch day.

What Phantom Edges are OFFICIAL

In the Phantom Blade series, Edges are the assassin's toolkit: quick, situational weapons that don't replace your main moveset but extend it. In Phantom Blade Zero they occupy two of your four loadout slots, so which Edges you bring is half of every build decision.

Combat roles OFFICIAL

Confirmed deployment contexts include mid-combo extension and stealth openers. Combined with the Sha-Chi resource system (parry, deflect, execution), Edges fill the gaps a main weapon can't: ranged pokes, area denial, and chain-starters.
