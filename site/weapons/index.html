The 30+ Main Weapons of Phantom Blade Zero

How acquisition, upgrades, new forms and reforging actually work.

Quick answer: Phantom Blade Zero has more than 30 main weapons (confirmed at the August 2026 State of Play). You carry two at once and swap between them mid-combat. Updated Oct 29 (launch day): the first 13 weapons now have real damage numbers, read from official demo / State of Play / preview footage — see the table below and the full sortable weapon database.

How you get weapons OFFICIAL

Main weapons are wrested from defeated foes rather than bought from a shop — the enemies you beat define the arsenal you build. This makes loadout planning part of exploration: which weapons you can assemble depends on which bosses and elites you've conquered.

Upgrades and new forms OFFICIAL

Each weapon is upgradeable, and upgrading unlocks new forms — not just bigger numbers but changed behavior. A weapon's evolved form can alter its moveset, which means "which form of weapon X" is a real build decision, not only "which weapon".

Reforging: no wasted materials OFFICIAL

The Reforging Talisman returns every material you invested in a weapon. There is no permanent upgrade trap: you can experiment freely, then pull materials out and re-invest when your loadout changes. This is why our future comparison tool treats materials as liquid, not sunk cost.

Named weapons so far SHOWN

Community breakdowns of official footage have already identified 10+ named weapons — Sanguine, Jagged Edge, Seamless Death, the Soft Snake Sword, and more. See each one's type and demonstrated behavior in the full confirmed weapons list.
