Every weapon in Phantom Blade Zero, one loadout at a time

30+ main weapons. 25 Phantom Edges. Four equipped slots. This guide covers every weapon system S-GAME has officially confirmed — and clearly marks what can only be verified after launch.

Launch: October 29, 2026 — 3M+ wishlists
Quick answer: Phantom Blade Zero has 55+ weapons — more than 30 switchable main weapons plus 25 secondary weapons called Phantom Edges. You equip two mains + two Edges at once and can swap mid-combat. Weapons are wrested from defeated foes, upgrade into new forms, and can be reforged with every material returned.
