# Phantom Blade Zero Pre-Order Guide — Which Edition to Buy, Prices, and Store Differences

*Updated October 4, 2026 · Prices and bonuses verified against the official S-GAME site, PlayStation Store, and Steam listing. No speculation.*

## The short answer

**Two editions, same game: Standard at $59.99 and Digital Deluxe at $69.99.** The Deluxe adds two exclusive outfits, a digital artbook, and the soundtrack — no gameplay content. Pre-order bonuses (Treasure Basin + Legacy) are **early unlocks you can earn by playing**, so nothing is locked behind a pre-order. There is **no early access**: everyone starts October 29.

If you just want the game: Standard. If you want the outfits and OST and the $10 / ¥60 premium feels fine: Deluxe.

## Are the pre-order bonuses worth it?

**Honest answer: they're a head start, not exclusive content.** Both storefronts state this explicitly:

- **Treasure Basin** (accessory): +10% Remnant Dust drop rate.
- **Legacy** (outfit): cosmetic.

Both "can also be obtained through in-game progression" — that wording is on the [official site](https://pbz.s-game.com/m/en-US), the [PlayStation Store listing](https://www.playstation.com/th-th/games/phantom-blade-zero), and the [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4115450/Phantom_Blade_Zero/). So pre-ordering buys you convenience on day one, not content anyone else can't reach. Pre-order because you're buying anyway, not to "lock in" bonuses.

Also worth knowing: **there is no early access period.** Pre-ordering any edition does not get you in before October 29. Digital pre-orders can typically be cancelled before launch per each store's policy.

## Where to pre-order (platform differences)

- **PS5 / PS5 Pro** — [PlayStation Store](https://www.playstation.com/th-th/games/phantom-blade-zero). Digital pre-orders open since Aug 11, 2026. The PS5 version is Pro Enhanced, supports Game Help, and PS5 has a 12-month console exclusivity window (no Xbox before late 2027).
- **PC — Steam** — [AppID 4115450](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4115450/Phantom_Blade_Zero/). The safe default: achievements, cloud saves, Family Sharing, refund policy. The page discloses **Denuvo DRM** on the PC version.
- **PC — Epic Games Store** — same game and editions; strongest option if you play Fortnite, since Epic's purchase promo has included Soul-themed Fortnite cosmetics (per community tracking sites — not an official S-GAME benefit, so verify on the Epic page before buying).
- **Mainland China PC** — also listed via WeGame / TapTap PC storefronts for the local ecosystem, same ¥268 / ¥328 pricing.

No store-exclusive weapons or campaign missions exist. The only meaningful choice is which launcher you want the game in.

## FAQ

**Is the Deluxe Edition worth $10 more?**
Only if you want the two exclusive outfits, artbook, and soundtrack. It contains zero gameplay content. If you're on the fence, the Standard edition is the complete game.

**Do I miss anything by NOT pre-ordering?**
No exclusive content. Treasure Basin and Legacy are early unlocks, earnable in-game. The only "loss" is starting day one without them.

**Which edition do streamers/speedrunners buy?**
Standard — the Treasure Basin pre-order accessory (+10% Remnant Dust) actually matters most for fast progression, and it's in both editions anyway.

**Is there a physical edition?**
In Japan (package edition) and mainland China (limited ¥1,199 collector's box, ~20,000 units, JD store). No global physical release announced.

**Can I still pre-order?**
Yes — all three stores are taking pre-orders until the October 29 worldwide unlock. See our [release date and unlock-times page](/release-date/) for the exact local time.
